## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Response
The design system positions comprehensive architectural renovation not merely as construction work, but as a high-precision, stress-free investment in living quality. It projects solid institutional credibility, craft precision, transparent accountability, and Mediterranean warmth. The emotional objective is total reassurance: homeowners and commercial clients must immediately perceive technical mastery, strict financial adherence, legal guarantees, and meticulous aesthetic standards.

### Design Movement: Modern Architectural Functionalism
The aesthetic unites the clarity of modern Swiss graphic design with structural architectural framing. 
- **Structural Rhythm:** Deliberate alternating rhythms of deep maritime tones and crisp light surfaces reflect the transition between raw blueprints and impeccably finished interiors.
- **Selective Focus:** Orange is exclusively reserved as an active conversion trigger; it is never decorative.
- **Social Proof Prominence:** Verifiable trust mechanisms (official insurance coverage, licenses, Google verified audits, warranties) are integrated into primary interface levels rather than hidden in footers.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture & Roles
- **Primary (`#0F3D4C` - Deep Petroleum Teal):** Represents technical rigor, engineering capability, and structural authority. Applied to hero sections, navigation bars, section headers, critical container blocks, and authoritative badges.
- **Secondary (`#F28C28` - Vivid Ochre / Safety Orange):** The system's conversion catalyst. Reserved strictly for primary action verbs, lead generation submissions, phone triggers, budget calculators, and critical notification badges.
- **Tertiary (`#0A252E` - Deep Abyssal Slate):** Provides maximum grounding contrast for footers, technical specification panels, and legal trust disclaimers.
- **Neutral Base (`#1E293B` - Deep Charcoal Slate):** The primary reading ink for maximum legibility and contrast against light backgrounds. Secondary reading copy utilizes `#475569`.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` & `#F5F6F7`):** Alternated purposefully to denote structural zoning. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) holds elevated interactive cards and user input modules, while architectural off-white (`#F5F6F7`) establishes content grouping baselines.

### Functional Application Rules
1. Never dilute the secondary accent (`#F28C28`) by applying it to non-actionable decorative strokes, icons, or background fills.
2. Form fields retain high contrast with `#FFFFFF` backgrounds and `#CBD5E1` structural borders, shifting to `#0F3D4C` on focus and `#F28C28` for validation errors or active interactive states.

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchy
Inter delivers neutral geometric balance and legibility across numeric data, estimates, and editorial content.

- **Display & Headings:** Dense, tightly tracked titles convey architectural permanence. Key values, lead statements, and metric guarantees utilize `letterSpacing: -0.025em` to project confidence.
- **Numerical Data & Microcopy:** Calculators, pricing baselines, timeline estimates, and ratings employ `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for precise alignment in tabular views.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-weight, slightly tracked uppercase or semi-bold labels guide the user cleanly through multi-step qualification forms.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy: 12-Column Technical Grid
The interface adopts a solid, predictable 12-column grid system with a maximum container width of `1280px`. The balance between high-density estimation tools and spacious project showcases is managed through strict vertical step rhythms (`space-xl` through `space-2xl`).

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters. Lead forms and quotation engines occupy prominent 4-to-5 column static rails alongside hero media and portfolio evidence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters. Interactive split views and calculators transition into sequenced modular blocks.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` page margins. Interactive elements adapt to thumb-reach zones; split-screen comparisons swap from horizontal scrubbing to tap-to-toggle or vertical stacking.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Crisp Surfaces
Depth is achieved through architectural edge delineation combined with diffuse ambient light, avoiding muddy drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F5F6F7` neutral canvas. Used for section breaks, comparison bases, and structural background zones.
- **Level 1 (Card Elevation):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a crisp edge border `1px solid rgba(15, 61, 76, 0.08)` and subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 61, 76, 0.06)`. Used for content cards, review units, and collapsed accordions.
- **Level 2 (Active Interactive / Raised Cards):** Lifted state for hovered cards, active input groups, and trust badges: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 61, 76, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky CTAs & Critical Modals):** Pinned conversion anchors, floating quotation bars, and lightboxes: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 61, 76, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

### Controlled Curvature
The shape language uses subtle corner smoothing (`roundedness: 1` = `0.25rem` / `4px` baseline) to maintain technical discipline without appearing clinical:
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, Badges:** `0.375rem` (`6px`) corner radius.
- **Interactive Cards & Modals:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pill Elements:** Reserved exclusively for status indicators (e.g., "Disponible para obra en Marzo", "Garantía 10 Años").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion CTA:** Solid `#F28C28` with `#FFFFFF` text (`label-md` or `label-lg`). Includes a subtle inner bevel or crisp shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(242, 140, 40, 0.2)`. On hover, color transitions to `#DD7A1B` with an elevated shadow `0 6px 16px rgba(242, 140, 40, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Authoritative Button:** Solid `#0F3D4C` with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for secondary inquiries, downloading dossiers, or scheduling technical visits. Hover: `#0A252E`.
- **Tertiary / Outline Button:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #0F3D4C`, text `#0F3D4C`. Hover: background fills with `rgba(15, 61, 76, 0.05)`.

### Trust Badges & Google Reviews Unit
- **Google Star Badge:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid rgba(15, 61, 76, 0.1)`, `0.375rem` border-radius. Features official Google 'G' icon, 5 stars rendered in `#FBBC04`, numerical rating (`5,0`), and total review volume link (`177 valoraciones en Google`) in `#1E293B` bold microcopy.
- **Technical Certifications Strip:** Monochromatic `#0F3D4C` trust icons (Colegio de Arquitectos, Seguro RC 1M€, Certificación Energética) with subtle `#E2E8F0` vertical divider lines.

### Before/After Interactive Split Slider
- Container fixed at standard aspect ratio (`16:9` or `4:3`) with `0.5rem` radius and hidden overflow.
- Divider bar: `2px solid #FFFFFF` with a central draggable circular handle (`40px` diameter) in `#0F3D4C` containing dual directional chevron indicators.
- Overlay badges: Floating semi-transparent pills (`rgba(15, 61, 76, 0.75)` backdrop blur) anchored at bottom-left ("ESTADO PREVIO") and bottom-right ("REFORMA EJECUTADA").

### Instant Estimation Calculator
- Segmented selection chips for renovation types (Piso Completo, Cocina, Baño, Local).
- Numeric slider controls for square meters with active track in `#0F3D4C` and draggable thumb in `#F28C28`.
- Live estimate output block: Background `#0F3D4C` with large tabular typography showing estimated range (e.g., `35.000€ - 42.000€`) and a primary `#F28C28` button labeled "Desglosar Presupuesto Detallado".

### Fast Lead Capture Form
- Single-column or balanced two-column layout.
- Field labels in `label-sm` (`#1E293B`) with micro-hints in `#64748B`.
- Inputs: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `body-md`. Focus state uses outline ring `2px solid #0F3D4C` and border color `#0F3D4C`.
- Privacy & Ley de Protección de Datos statement in `body-sm` (`#64748B`) with explicit opt-in checkbox.

### Clean FAQ Accordion
- Border bottom only (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- Trigger: `headline-sm` font weight, `#0F3D4C` color, full width, flex-aligned with a rotating chevron indicator.
- Open state: Panel slides down smoothly; content rendered in `body-md` (`#334155`) with a maximum reading width of `720px`.

### Pinned Sticky Action Bar (Mobile & Desktop)
- **Mobile Sticky Footer Bar:** Pinned bottom bar (`64px` height) with `#FFFFFF` background, top border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and elevation level 3. Contains a split layout: Direct Call trigger (Icon + "Llamar ahora" in `#0F3D4C`) and full CTA ("Pedir Presupuesto" in `#F28C28`).
- **Desktop Floating Contact Rail:** Unobtrusive floating panel pinned to the right edge with fast contact triggers (WhatsApp, Presupuesto Express, Teléfono Directo).
